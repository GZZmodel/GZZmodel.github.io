<div style='text-align: justify;'>
The Generalized Zhang-Zhu (GZZ) criterion is a true 3D Hoek-Brown criterion proposed by Dr. Lianyang Zhang and Dr. Hehua Zhu. This criterion has been recommended as a standard by the International Society for Rock Mechanics (ISRM). 
</div>
<div style='text-align: justify;'>
The advantages of the GZZ criterion lies on three main aspects:
</div>
<div style='text-align: justify;'>
Scientificity: reflects the effect of intermediate principal stress on rock mass strength, reveals dual non-linearity of rock strength, brittle-ductile failure transition, failure mode on both π and meridian planes.
</div>
<div style='text-align: justify;'>
Applicability: share the same parameters with HB criterion, parameters comes directly from site, combining with digital in-situ tests, can achieve precise and dynamic design of tunnel surrounded by rock mass.
</div>
<div style='text-align: justify;'>
Practices in deep engineering: uncover unified stress-induced mechanism of rock engineering catastrophe failure, including tensile-shear failure, rock burst, large deformation, provide theoretical support for rock engineering catastrophe failure prediction and prevention.
</div>
