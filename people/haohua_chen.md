<div style='text-align: justify;'>
  Dr. Haohua Chen is currently an associate Professor in Department of Geotechnical Engineering, College of Civil Engineering at Tongji University. He obtained his bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from Beihang University in 2015, master’s degree in Geotechnical Engineering from Tongji University in 2018, and completed his Ph.D. degree from the University of Arizona in Civil Engineering and Engineering Mechanics in 2022, under the guidance of Prof. Lianyang Zhang. He then joined the laboratory of Prof. Ingrid Tomac at the University of California, San Diego as a postdoctoral fellow, where his research focused on energy geotechnics and numerical simulations using coupled CFD-DEM. Dr. Chen received the Excellent Young Scientist fund (Oversee) in 2023 and started as an associate professor at Tongji University in 2024. His current research interest focuses on rock mechanics and underground engineering.
</div>
