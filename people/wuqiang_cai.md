<div style='text-align: justify;'>
  Dr. Wuqiang Cai was born in October 1989 in Shehong, Sichuan. He graduated from the School of Civil Engineering of Tongji University (majoring in tunnel and underground construction), with a doctoral degree in engineering obtained through postgraduate studies. Currently, he serves as an associate professor at the School of Transportation of Tongji University.
Under the guidance of Academician Liang Wenhao and Academician Zhu Hehua of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, he engages in research on the design theory of deep rock mass mechanics and underground engineering. His achievements were listed as one of the top ten scientific breakthroughs of Chinese universities by the journal Science in 2020. In 2025, he won the First Prize for Scientific and Technological Innovation Achievements of the China Industry-University-Research Cooperation Promotion Association (a provincial and ministerial-level first prize). In 2022, he won the Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award of the Chinese Society for Rock Mechanics and Engineering (CSRME) (ranked first). He was also nominated for the "Rocha Award" of the International Society for Rock Mechanics and Rock Engineering (ISRM), and won the Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award of Tongji University as well as the Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award in Hydraulic Rock Mechanics in China. He has published more than 40 high-level SCI and EI papers, and has applied for/been authorized 15 national invention patents.
</div>
