<div style='text-align: justify;'>
  Dr. Qi Zhang is a professor at Southeast University. From 2001 to 2008, he completed his undergraduate and master's studies at China University of Mining and Technology and obtained a master's degree in Geotechnical Engineering under the supervision of Professor Zhou Guoqing. From 2008 to 2013, he obtained a doctoral degree in Tunnel and Underground Construction Engineering at Tongji University, supervised by Prof. Zhu Hehua. From 2009 to 2010 and from 2012 to 2013, he studied as a visiting scholar in the Department of Civil Engineering and Mechanics at the University of Arizona for two years, supervised by Prof. Lianyang Zhang. Since 2014, he has been teaching at the School of Civil Engineering, Southeast University. Currently, he serves as the Assistant Dean of the Institute of Future Underground Space, Southeast University.
He has published more than 40 SCI/EI-indexed papers as the first author or corresponding author in international journals such as TUST, IJRMMS, RMRE, as well as in the Chinese Journal of Rock Mechanics and Engineering. Among them, the highest single paper has been cited 239 times by others. He has published 2 monographs and participated in compiling 3 monographs. He has been authorized 12 invention patents and applied for 10 invention patents. He has also served as the chief editor of the group standard of the Chinese Rock Mechanics and Engineering Society, "Information Model of Rock Tunnel Engineering". He won the Special Award for Natural Science of the 12th Chinese Rock Mechanics and Engineering Society (ranked third), and was selected into the "Future Leadership Program" (ARMA Future Leadership) of the American Rock Mechanics Association in 2023. His doctoral thesis won the Excellent Postgraduate Achievements (Doctoral Dissertation) Award in Shanghai and the Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award of the Chinese Rock Mechanics and Engineering Society.
